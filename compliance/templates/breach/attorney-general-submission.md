# Sample copy to the California Attorney General (checklist)

> **Draft for counsel.** Register CA-004-005: when one breach requires notice to more than 500 California residents, a single sample copy of the notice, with no personal information, is submitted electronically to the Attorney General. The register records the deadline as 15 calendar days after residents are notified (SB 446). Counsel must confirm the deadline and the submission method.

- [ ] Count the California residents notified for this breach. Is it more than 500? If not, no AG submission is needed under this rule.
- [ ] Prepare one sample of the notice exactly as sent, with every name, address and number removed.
- [ ] Get counsel's approval.
- [ ] Submit it through the Attorney General's online data-breach form (counsel confirms the current link).
- [ ] Record the submission date and the confirmation in the incident log.
- [ ] Deadline: [date residents were notified] + 15 calendar days = [date].
