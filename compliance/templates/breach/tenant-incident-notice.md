# Security incident notice to a contractor business (template)

> **Draft for counsel.** Volteira sends this to an affected contractor business (tenant) **immediately** after discovering that data it holds for them may have been acquired by an unauthorized person (register CA-004-006 and SEC-006-008). The tenant may own the data and may have its own duty to notify its customers. This notice gives them the facts they need to decide. Counsel approves it before it is sent.

Subject: Security incident affecting your Volteira account — action may be needed

[Owner name],

On [date and time] we found that [a plain description of what happened]. We are telling you now, before our investigation is finished, so that you have what you need to meet any duty you have to your own customers.

**What we know so far**
- When it happened: [date or range, or "not yet known"]
- What was involved: [the records and fields, for example customer names and phone numbers, job notes, photos, invoices]
- How many of your customers may be affected: [number, or "still being determined"]
- Whether any payment card numbers, bank details or passwords were involved: [yes/no/unknown] (card payments are handled by Stripe, and Volteira does not store card numbers)

**What we have done**
[Containment: keys rotated, the access closed, accounts secured, logs preserved.]

**What you may need to do**
California law may require a business that owns personal information to notify its customers after a breach. Please speak with your own attorney about whether that applies to you. We will provide the facts, a list of the affected customers and their contact details, and help sending notices if you need it.

**Next update**: by [date/time]. Contact for questions: [name, phone, email].

Shadam LLC (Volteira)
