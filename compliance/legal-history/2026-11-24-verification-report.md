## Judgement calls

- **CalOPPA (CA-002-001…009, WEB-CA-001…008) left unverified.** The only full text of B&P 22575-22579 on an allowed host is an Attorney General / Office of Privacy Protection reprint dated 2014 (22575) or 2008 (22576-22577). It was read and is consistent, and corrections it clearly supports were applied (e.g. the 22577(b) conspicuous-posting methods), but a decade-old reprint cannot establish current law, so `source_verified` stays false until leginfo can be read.
- **eCFR renderer API URLs are cited** as `official_source_url` because that is the exact official page read; the human eCFR pages are blocked from the audit environment.
- **Items with several authorities** are verified only when every authority the required behavior depends on was read; partial reads are recorded in the notes.
- **Code impact.** Corrections change register text only. Several touch money/consent rules that the app implements (B&P 7159.5 down payment, 7159 notices, CCPA opt-out timing, 47 CFR 64.1200 revocation); those should be re-checked against the code and templates in a follow-up audit.
